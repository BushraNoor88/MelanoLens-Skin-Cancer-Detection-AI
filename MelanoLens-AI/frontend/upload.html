Tips for good results

  • Use a sharply focused, evenly lit dermoscopic image.
  • Images with large hairs, rulers or markers may need the hair-reduction option.
  • A reference mask enables Dice, IoU and F1 metrics on the Evaluation page.
  • Files are validated here and again on the server; larger images are resized for processing.
