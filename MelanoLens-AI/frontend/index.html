Platform

A complete boundary analysis workbench

From validated upload to measured contours and reference-mask metrics, every step runs locally and every number comes from the saved results.

Safe image intake

JPG and PNG uploads are validated in the browser and again on the server, with an optional reference mask for evaluation.

Image processing

Gaussian, median and bilateral filters, CLAHE contrast, hair-artifact reduction and resizing, all adjustable per run.

Edge detection

Canny, Sobel, Prewitt, Laplacian and Laplacian of Gaussian, each computed conventionally and with the Edge Booster.

Boundary extraction

Gap closing, contour tracing, area filtering and candidate selection with area, perimeter and circularity measurements.

Evaluation

Dice, IoU, precision, recall, F1 and boundary F1 against your reference mask, with CSV and JSON export.

Private by design

Everything runs on your computer. Nothing is sent to third-party services, and stored results can be deleted at any time.

Research

Algorithms under study

Classical operators with transparent mathematics. Nothing is a black box.

Edge detectors

Canny detector

Gaussian smoothing, gradient, non-maximum suppression and two-threshold hysteresis give thin, connected edges.

Sobel detector

3×3 derivative kernels give Gx and Gy; magnitude sqrt(Gx² + Gy²) is thresholded.

Prewitt detector

Like Sobel but with equal weights in the kernel rows, applied with explicit convolution.

Laplacian detector

Second derivative; edges are where intensity curvature is strong. Sensitive to noise.

Laplacian of Gaussian optional

Gaussian smoothing first, then the Laplacian, which tames noise at the cost of fine detail.

Edge Booster methods

Sobel gradient

I' = normalize(I + α·G)

Adds the normalised gradient back to the image. Large α amplifies noise.

Laplacian sharpening

I' = I − s·∇²I

Unsharp-style sharpening using the Laplacian with a negative centre.

Morphological gradient

dilate(I) − erode(I)

Edge thickness from dilation minus erosion; kernel size sets the scale.

Multi-scale experimental

Sobel responses at several blur scales, each normalised, then combined with weights.

Classical vision meets CNNs

Run all three paths on the same image and compare them with real metrics. A CNN is not assumed to be better.

Does edge enhancement really help?

Upload an image, optionally with a reference mask, and investigate it with real computed numbers. Enhancement can make edges look stronger without making the segmentation more accurate; the app reports both outcomes as they are.
